Targets that learn from you.

MacroCraft watches what you actually eat and how your weight actually moves, works out how much energy you really use, and adjusts your targets once a week from that evidence — never from a single heavy morning.

Log quickly

Thousands of foods that work offline, barcode scanning, recipes with variants, and copying a meal or a whole day you ate before. Describe a meal in your own words or photograph a label, and review what was understood before anything is logged.

The trend, not the noise

A smoothed weight trend beside every weigh-in, so a salty dinner does not look like fat gain. Expenditure, intake and body composition over a week, a month or years — every chart readable point by point, including with VoiceOver.

A weekly check-in

Targets for losing, gaining or maintaining, kept within safe limits on calories and rate of change. When the evidence is thin, MacroCraft says so instead of guessing.

Private by design

  • No account, no app server, no analytics. There is nowhere for your data to arrive.
  • Encrypted on your phone. Your history never leaves it unless you export it.
  • Apple Health, metric by metric. Weight, steps and body composition only if you allow each one.
  • Backups only you can open, protected by a recovery key nobody else holds.
