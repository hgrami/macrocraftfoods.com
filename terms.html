What MacroCraft is

MacroCraft is nutrition and weight-management software. It estimates your energy expenditure from what you log and how your weight trends, and suggests calorie and macronutrient targets.

It is not medical advice. Its targets, safety floors and rate limits are guardrails in a piece of software. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, or have a history of disordered eating. Stop using a target that does not feel right for you.

Your data

Everything you enter is stored on your device, encrypted, and is not sent to us. The privacy policy describes exactly what the app stores and the few requests it makes.

Because there is no account and no server, we cannot recover your data. Keeping a backup, and keeping its recovery key, is your responsibility. If you lose your device, your recovery key or a backup file, we have no copy to give back.

Food data

The bundled food database comes from public sources (Health Canada's Canadian Nutrient File and USDA FoodData Central), and barcode lookups come from Open Food Facts. Nutrition values from any source, including a label you scan, can be wrong or out of date. Check a value that matters to you.
